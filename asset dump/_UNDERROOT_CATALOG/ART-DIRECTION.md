# UNDERROOT Art Direction

## Recommended visual identity

UNDERROOT should read as **reclaimed infrastructure being overtaken by resilient life**: warm soil and rust against cool cave shadows, with bioluminescent accents used as navigation and danger signals.

## Palette

- Base: deep charcoal, desaturated soil brown, moss green.
- Structure: oxidized orange, dirty cream, muted steel blue.
- Life: leaf green and fungal red/magenta.
- Signal: crystal cyan and acid yellow-green, used sparingly.

## Pixel and rendering rules

1. Anchor gameplay geometry at 16x16 and render with nearest-neighbour filtering.
2. Use 32x32 Woods tiles either at half logical scale or as a deliberately larger environmental layer.
3. Keep sprite outlines and shadow direction consistent; do not combine PostApoc shadow and no-shadow assets casually.
4. Preserve source proportions in previews and in-game; never stretch non-uniformly.
5. Use animation for environmental feedback: water/acid/lava surfaces, fireflies, torches, crystal glow, and machinery.
6. Use parallax backgrounds for depth, not as collision surfaces.

## Strongest matches

1. **Underground Cave and Crystal** ? strongest technical foundation: documented 16x16 scale, metadata, native assets, animations, and clear commercial-use terms.
2. **Woods** ? strongest greenhouse/overgrowth source: documented 32x32 tiles, 512x288 layered backgrounds, and vegetation decorations.
3. **PostApoc Workshop** ? strongest industrial bridge: 16x16 slicing guidance, room tiles, icons, and shadow variants.
4. **IndustrialPunk** ? strong thematic match for rusted machinery, but licence and exact scale need confirmation.
5. **Sci-fi Planet Add-ons** ? useful mood/reference images only until provenance is established.
